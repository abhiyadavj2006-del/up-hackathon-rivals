About the event

Where talent meets impact.

Up Hackathon Rivals is a national innovation platform that brings together students, academicians, startups, and industry leaders to solve the challenges that matter most to society. From idea validation to prototype build-outs, the program helps teams turn bold concepts into useful, scalable solutions.

Whether your focus is AI, sustainability, healthtech, productivity, or creative technology, this is the place to build, learn, and pitch your vision on a national stage.
